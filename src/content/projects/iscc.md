The International Standard Content Code (ISCC) is a content-derived identifier for digital
media assets. Unlike traditional identifiers that are assigned by a registry, ISCCs are
generated from the content itself using similarity-preserving algorithms.

ISCC was published as **ISO 24138:2024** by the International Organization for
Standardization. It supports text, image, audio, and video content types.

Key properties:

- **Content-derived** — the identifier is computed from the asset, not assigned
- **Similarity-preserving** — similar content produces similar codes
- **Multi-modal** — works across text, image, audio, and video
- **Decentralized** — no central registry required for generation
