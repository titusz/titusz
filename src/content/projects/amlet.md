Amlet provides a global TDM (Text and Data Mining) registry that enables publishers to
declare AI usage rights in a machine-readable format. Built on ISCC technology, Amlet
bridges the gap between content creators and AI developers by making licensing terms
discoverable and actionable.

The platform supports the EU AI Act and DSM Directive requirements for rights reservations,
providing a standards-based approach to content licensing in the AI era.
