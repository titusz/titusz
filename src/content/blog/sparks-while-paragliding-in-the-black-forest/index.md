![Charcoal sketch of a paraglider pilot with purple electrical sparks crackling around outstretched hands, storm clouds nearby](/images/sparks-paragliding-hero.webp)

Today I was out paragliding in the Black Forest. The flying site is in the south-western part
of the Black Forest near Freiburg, Germany, at the Elztal. The take-off site is called
Gschasi (or Hans Uhl).

A very strange and mysterious thing happened during this flight. While holding my digicam to
take some pictures of the clouds — which had an icy structure on top — some sparks started
sizzling around my gloves and the camera. The spectacle held on for about 5 seconds and I was
quite astounded by that mini-lightning. I didn't feel anything like an electric shock in my
fingers and I'm not sure what the phenomenon was. It didn't look like the camera produced the
effect. I had to assume the nearby clouds were related to this event — they were about to
evolve into a storm. Maybe it was [St. Elmo's fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Elmo%27s_fire).

The digicam is still working fine. The only strange thing is that it produced a completely
black picture and another picture I can't identify, both captured at about the time the sparks
flashed around.
