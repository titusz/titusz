After years of building tools and standards for digital media, I'm finally starting a
personal blog. The motivation is simple: I want a place to share thoughts that don't fit
into academic papers, project documentation, or social media posts.

## What to expect

I'll write about topics I work on daily:

- **Content identification** — how we can create persistent, decentralized identifiers for
  digital content across media types
- **Open standards** — the process and politics of building ISO standards, and why
  interoperability matters
- **AI and content rights** — the intersection of machine learning, text and data mining
  regulations, and content licensing
- **Software engineering** — Python, distributed systems, and the tools I use to build
  things

## Why now?

The ISCC has been published as ISO 24138:2024, Amlet is growing, and there's more to
discuss than ever. The AI era is raising fundamental questions about content provenance,
attribution, and licensing. I want to contribute to that conversation beyond the scope of
my day-to-day work.
